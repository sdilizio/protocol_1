Therapy that starts with what a good day looks like for you.

Occupational therapy helps people take part in the everyday activities that give life its shape: caring for yourself, keeping a home, working, connecting with others.

I'm Joanne, a registered occupational therapist in Brisbane. I see clients where life actually happens, take time to listen, and work closely with families, carers and support teams to make change that lasts.

Joanne

How I can help

Every plan is tailored. These are the areas I work in most often.

  • Functional assessments

    An honest picture of strengths and needs, with recommendations you can act on.

  • Home modifications

    Practical changes, from grab rails to bathroom redesigns, that make home safer.

  • Assistive technology

    Choosing, trialling and setting up equipment that suits you and your space.

  • Daily living skills

    Routines and strategies for self-care, cooking, and getting out and about.

  • NDIS supports

    Capacity building and clear, well-evidenced reports for plan reviews.

  • Mobile visits

    Sessions at home, at work or in the community across Brisbane.

Working together

  1. Get in touchRefer yourself, or ask your GP, support coordinator or family to reach out.
  2. We meetAn initial assessment, usually at home, to understand your goals.
  3. A clear planRecommendations shaped around your priorities and budget.
  4. Therapy & reviewRegular sessions and check-ins as things change.

Funding

NDIS (self- and plan-managed), Medicare with a GP plan, private health insurance, DVA and private clients.
