About

Occupational therapy is about the ordinary parts of life: getting ready in the morning, cooking a meal, getting out into the community, doing the work you love.

I'm Joanne, a registered occupational therapist based in Brisbane. I started OTwithJoanne to offer therapy that is unhurried and personal. I come to you, take the time to understand what a good day looks like for you, and work alongside you and the people who support you to get there.

Services

  • Functional assessments

    A clear picture of your strengths and needs at home and in the community, with practical recommendations.

  • Home modifications

    Rails, ramps, bathroom changes and layout advice so your home works for you.

  • Assistive equipment

    Trialling, prescribing and setting up the right equipment, from shower chairs to mobility aids.

  • Daily living skills

    Building routines and skills for self-care, meal preparation and getting around.

  • NDIS reports

    Thorough, well-evidenced reports for plan reviews and funding requests.

  • Mobile visits

    Appointments in your home, workplace or community across Brisbane.

How it works

  1. Get in touchCall or email. You can refer yourself, or be referred by your GP, support coordinator or family.
  2. Initial assessmentWe meet where life happens, usually at home, to talk about your goals and see what's getting in the way.
  3. A plan togetherYou receive clear recommendations and a plan that fits your priorities and budget.
  4. Therapy and reviewWe work towards your goals and check in regularly so the plan keeps up with you.
