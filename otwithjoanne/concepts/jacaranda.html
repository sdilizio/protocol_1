Joanne Di LizioRegistered Occupational Therapist

Hi, I'm Joanne.

I'm a registered occupational therapist based in Brisbane. OT is about the everyday: getting dressed, making a cuppa, getting to the shops, going back to work. When those things get harder, I help you find a way.

I visit you at home or wherever you need support, listen first, and work alongside you, your family and your support team to build a plan that genuinely fits your life.

How I can help

A few of the things I do most. Not sure if OT is right for you? Just ask.

  • Functional assessments

    A clear picture of what's working and what isn't, with practical next steps.

  • Home modifications

    Rails, ramps and bathroom changes so home stays safe and comfortable.

  • Equipment

    Finding, trialling and setting up the right assistive equipment for you.

  • Daily living skills

    Routines for self-care, meals and getting out and about with confidence.

  • NDIS reports

    Thorough, well-evidenced reports for plan reviews and funding requests.

  • I come to you

    Visits at home, at work or in the community, right across Brisbane.

What to expect

  1. Say helloCall or email. Self-referrals are welcome, as are referrals from GPs and coordinators.
  2. First visitWe meet, usually at home, to talk about your goals and what's getting in the way.
  3. Your planClear recommendations that fit your priorities and budget.
  4. Keep goingTherapy sessions and regular check-ins as things change.

Ways to fund your therapy

  • NDIS (self- and plan-managed)
  • Medicare with a GP plan
  • Private health insurance
  • DVA
  • Private clients
